AI-First Digital Strategy, Built on the Ground in Southern Oregon

Most digital agencies along the I-5 corridor pitch some version of the same playbook: build a website, run ads, wait for the phone to ring. Leverage AI starts from a different premise entirely. Mike Schlottig, who founded the studio in 2025, noticed that the businesses winning local work in 2026 aren't the ones with the biggest ad budgets — they're the ones that show up when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overview who can solve their problem in their town.

That observation turned into a practice. From a home office on Bellevue Place in Grants Pass, Schlottig builds what he calls "local demand systems" — connected infrastructure that ties together a business's search visibility, website credibility, lead capture, and follow-up speed into a single measurable pipeline. He works directly with service businesses across the Rogue Valley and the broader Oregon I-5 corridor, from HVAC contractors in Medford to organic suppliers right here in Grants Pass.

What makes the approach unusual is the tooling. Schlottig is a systems architect by background, not a marketer who learned to blog. He writes the schema markup himself, builds custom Cloudflare Worker infrastructure for his clients' websites, designs entity-strength architectures that make businesses machine-readable to AI systems, and deploys lead-response automation that fires before a prospect's attention cools. Every engagement starts with a diagnostic audit that maps exactly where a business leaks demand.

The studio operates on a no-lock-in model — no long-term contracts, no retainers with vague deliverables. Schlottig takes appointments Monday through Saturday and works directly with every client. There's no account manager, no handoff to a junior associate. If you're on a call with Leverage AI, you're talking to the person who's going to do the work.

For Oregon service businesses that have outgrown template websites and generic SEO advice, this is a different kind of partner — one that treats local search visibility, AI discoverability, and website performance as engineering problems, not marketing ones.
