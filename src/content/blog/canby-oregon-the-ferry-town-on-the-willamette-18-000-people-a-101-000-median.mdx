## The Ferry Still Runs

The Canby Ferry has been crossing the Willamette River since 1914. The current vessel, the *M.J. Lee II* — named for the first child born in Canby (Millard Jerome Lee, born 1872) — has been in service since 1997. It's a cable ferry powered by electricity from overhead lines, guided by an underwater cable, carrying six cars and 49 passengers at a time.

It is one of only three remaining ferries on the Willamette River, alongside the Buena Vista Ferry and the Wheatland Ferry. In a state that has bridged, dammed, and paved over most of its river crossings, the Canby Ferry is a working anachronism — a $4 car ride across the Willamette that connects Canby to Wilsonville and Stafford on the west bank.

The ferry is not a tourist attraction in the traditional sense. It's a commute route, a shortcut, and a civic institution that Canby maintains as a point of pride and practical transportation.

## Agricultural Roots, Suburban Growth

Canby was platted in 1870 and has grown from a farming community into a small city of approximately 17,931, per World Population Review. The city sits in Clackamas County, 5 miles east of I-5, connected to the interstate via Highway 99E and local roads through Wilsonville and Aurora.

The **median household income is $100,989** with a poverty rate of 7.95%. The median age is 43 — older and more established than many corridor towns, reflecting a community of homeowners and families who have been here for decades.

Canby's economy mixes agriculture (the nursery and greenhouse industry is significant in Clackamas County), light manufacturing, and the economic gravity of the Portland metro. The city promotes itself as business-friendly, noting its accessibility: I-5 is 5 miles away, I-205 is 9 miles east via an uncongested route, and Portland International Airport is reachable via the same corridor.

## Getting to Canby

Canby is located 5 miles east of I-5. The nearest interstate access is **I-5 Exits 282B-283** (Wilsonville), with connections via Wilsonville Road and Highway 99E. The Canby Ferry provides a direct crossing to the west bank of the Willamette.

Canby is 25 miles south of Portland, 15 miles southwest of Oregon City, and 5 miles east of Wilsonville.
