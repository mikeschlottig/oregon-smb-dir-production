## The Southern Oregon Corridor

The I-5 journey through Oregon begins at the California border, climbing through the Siskiyou Pass at 4,310 feet before descending into the Rogue Valley — a sun-drenched, mountain-rimmed basin that feels more like Northern California than the rainy Oregon of popular imagination.

This corridor covers 60 miles and eight communities, from Ashland's world-class theater scene to Grants Pass's river-town swagger. Along the way, you'll pass through some of Oregon's most economically diverse small towns — places where Shakespeare coexists with agriculture, where gold rush history meets modern wine tasting, and where the Rogue River defines everything.

## Corridor Themes

- **Fire & Resilience:** The 2020 Almeda Fire reshaped Talent and Phoenix, and the recovery continues to define both communities.

- **The Rogue River:** From Gold Hill to Grants Pass, the Rogue River is the corridor's defining natural feature — drawing rafters, anglers, and jet boat operators.

- **Cultural Extremes:** Ashland's Shakespeare Festival and Medford's commercial economy represent opposite poles of a valley that contains both.

- **Wine & Agriculture:** The Rogue Valley AVA produces award-winning wines alongside pears, cattle, and hay.
