## The Umpqua to Eugene Corridor

This corridor marks the great transition on the I-5 journey. Heading north from Sutherlin, you leave the canyon country and timber economy of Douglas County and enter Lane County — home to the University of Oregon, TrackTown USA, and the beginning of the broad, flat Willamette Valley that defines central Oregon.

Eleven communities span this 60-mile stretch, from tiny Oakland (population 1,000) to Eugene (population 179,000). The contrast is the story.

## Town-by-Town Guide

### 19. [🪵 Sutherlin, Oregon: The Timber Town That Kept Growing After the Mills Went Quiet](/blog/sutherlin-oregon-the-timber-town-that-kept-growing-after-the-mills-went-quiet/)

**Pop. ~8,700 | Exit 136 | Narrative Economist**

Timber heritage town transitioning to a bedroom community for Roseburg, with steady growth and affordable housing.

### 20. [🧱 Oakland, Oregon: The First City on the State Historic Register, and the Two-Block Downtown That Time Forgot to Demolish](/blog/oakland-oregon-the-first-city-on-the-state-historic-register-and-the-two-block/)

**Pop. ~1,000 | Exit 138 | Essayist-Witness**

Oregon's first National Historic District — a preserved 19th-century town that chose to keep its character.

### 21. Rice Hill

**Unincorporated | Exit 148 | Curious Empiricist**

The K&R Drive-In, 40 flavors of Umpqua ice cream, and a classic I-5 road trip stop.

### 22. Yoncalla

**Pop. ~1,041 | Exit 150 | Moral Cartographer**

Named for the local Kalapuya band, home of Jesse Applegate — the "Sage of Yoncalla" — and the Applegate Trail.

### 23. Drain

**Pop. ~1,200 | Exit 162 | Conversational Philosopher**

Gateway to the coast via OR-38, Pass Creek covered bridge, and the site of Oregon's last California Condor sighting (1904).

### 24. Cottage Grove

**Pop. ~10,700 | Exits 170, 174 | Pattern Detective**

The "Covered Bridges Capital of Oregon," Row River Trail, Bohemia Mining District, filming location for *Stand By Me*.

### 25. [🌻 Creswell, Oregon: The Friendly City at the Edge of Lane County's Orbit](/blog/creswell-oregon-the-friendly-city-at-the-edge-of-lane-county-s-orbit/)

**Pop. ~5,600 | Exit 182 | Conversational Philosopher**

"The Friendly City" — affordable Lane County living, Oregon Country Fair gateway, home of Mark Few.

### 26. [🔀 Goshen, Oregon: The Junction Where I-5 Meets the Road to the Cascades](/blog/goshen-oregon-the-junction-where-i-5-meets-the-road-to-the-cascades/)

**Pop. ~2,300 | Exit 186 | Conversational Philosopher**

The fork in the road — where I-5 meets OR-58 to Oakridge, Willamette Pass, and the Cascades.

### 27. [🍩 Springfield, Oregon: The Real Springfield — Where the Simpsons Were Born, the McKenzie Runs Clean, and 61,000 People Finally Get Their Due](/blog/springfield-oregon-the-real-springfield-where-the-simpsons-were-born-the/)

**Pop. ~61,200 | Exits 191, 194, 195 | Pattern Detective**

The real Springfield (*The Simpsons*), McKenzie River city, Weyerhaeuser heritage, Oregon's #1 moving destination.

### 28. [🏟️ Eugene, Oregon: TrackTown, College Town, Counterculture Capital — The City That Runs on Ideas and Literally Invented Jogging](/blog/eugene-oregon-tracktown-college-town-counterculture-capital-the-city-that-runs/)

**Pop. ~179,000 | Exits 192-199 | Pattern Detective**

TrackTown USA, University of Oregon, Hayward Field, the city that invented jogging, counterculture capital, world-class food scene.

## Corridor Themes

- **The Great Transition:** From timber-dependent Douglas County to university-driven Lane County.

- **Covered Bridges:** Cottage Grove is the epicenter, but the entire corridor celebrates Oregon's bridge heritage.

- **Running Culture:** Eugene's identity as TrackTown USA — Bowerman, Prefontaine, Nike, Hayward Field — defines the corridor's northern anchor.

- **The Willamette Valley Begins:** South of Eugene, the flat, fertile valley opens up — the agricultural heartland that feeds the rest of the state.
