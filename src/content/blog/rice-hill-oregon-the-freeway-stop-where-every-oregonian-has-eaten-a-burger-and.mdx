## The Tradition You Can't Explain

Ask any Oregonian who has driven I-5 between Portland and Southern Oregon about Rice Hill, and you'll get the same response: a slight nod, a half-smile, and something like "Oh yeah, that's where we always stop."

Rice Hill is not a town. It's barely a place. It's an unincorporated dot at Exit 148, halfway between Roseburg and Eugene, with a truck plaza, a motel, and a drive-in restaurant. There is no downtown. There is no city government. There is no reason, on paper, for anyone to stop here.

And yet everyone does.

[K&R Drive Inn](https://thatoregonlife.com/2024/09/k-and-r-drive-in-rice-hill-oakland/) has been operating at 201 John Long Road since 1970. It is a family-run burger stand with outdoor seating, 40 flavors of Umpqua Dairy ice cream, and a menu that hasn't changed much in 55 years. Burgers, fries, milkshakes, chili dogs, root beer floats. Nothing fancy. Everything right.

The internet confirms what Oregonians already know. [Reddit threads](https://www.reddit.com/r/oregon/comments/1j6jwry/best_food_stops_along_i5_going_through_oregon/) about the best food stops on I-5 inevitably mention K&R. [That Oregon Life](https://thatoregonlife.com/2024/09/k-and-r-drive-in-rice-hill-oakland/) called it "the old-fashioned drive-in restaurant in Oregon that hasn't changed in decades." Truckers call in orders from the highway so the food is ready when they pull in. Families with kids make it a scheduled stop on every north-south road trip.

The bacon cheeseburger is the move. The root beer float is the closer. And the 40 flavors of Umpqua ice cream are the reason you'll stand in line on a summer Saturday.

## Why Here?

Rice Hill's location is the answer. Exit 148 sits almost exactly halfway between Eugene and Roseburg — roughly 50 miles in each direction. For anyone driving the full I-5 corridor through Oregon, it's the natural midpoint between the Willamette Valley and the Umpqua. The Rice Hill Truck Plaza reinforces the stop with fuel, parking, and a restaurant. K&R provides the reason to linger.

The unincorporated community was named for the Rice family, early settlers in the area. Beyond the exit, there's nothing but Douglas County forest and farmland. That emptiness is part of the charm — Rice Hill is a pause between places, and the pause has become the tradition.

## Getting to Rice Hill

**I-5 Exit 148** provides access to Rice Hill. K&R Drive Inn is at 201 John Long Road, visible from the exit. Open Tuesday through Friday 11:30 AM–6 PM (until 7 PM Thursday–Friday), and Sunday 11:30 AM–6 PM. Closed Saturday.

Rice Hill is 25 miles north of Roseburg, 50 miles south of Eugene, and approximately 155 miles south of Portland on I-5.
