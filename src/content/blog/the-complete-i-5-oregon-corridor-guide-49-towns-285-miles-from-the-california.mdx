## Every Town on I-5 in Oregon. Every Story Worth Telling.

Interstate 5 is Oregon's spine — the 308-mile north-south corridor that connects the California border to the Columbia River, threading through mountain passes, canyon gorges, timber country, the Willamette Valley's agricultural heartland, the state capital, and the Portland metro.

This guide profiles **every community along the I-5 corridor in Oregon** — 49 towns and cities, from Ashland's Shakespeare Festival to Portland's food carts. Each article is independently researched, locally sourced, and written in a distinct voice that matches the character of the place.

The corridor is organized into five segments, each with its own character, economy, and story.
